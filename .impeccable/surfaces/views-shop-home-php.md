# Storefront + Admin — surface brief

Scope: whole site. Storefront `views/shop/` = Persuade; admin `views/admin/` = Operate, same world in a quiet register.
Audience/job: Dasmariñas households buying vegetables, fruit, meat, eggs and rice on phones; two admins (EJ, Kiel) running orders, stock, and prices on phone and desktop.
Constraints: customer + admin only (no packer/driver/trips); English copy with Filipino produce and dish names only; real free-license photos with recorded credits; no build step (vanilla CSS/JS); prefers-reduced-motion honored.
Memorable moment: Add stamps a flexo lot number onto the sack label and the basket count ticks like a scale numeral.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a printed sako ng bigas: every surface is woven poly sack printed in two flexo inks. It refuses the leafy green-and-white organic-grocery grid.

OWN-WORLD: Sack white #F4F1E8 with a fine woven texture, flexo red #C8202F, field green #1F6B3A, ink #161512. Slight ink misregistration on display type, halftone dots, a stitched-thread seam as header, footer and dividers. Archivo variable: ultra-condensed black for giant KG net-weight stamps, normal width for UI, tabular price figures. Inline-SVG sack badge seal with rice stalks. Admin: same two inks on flat sack white, no weave on data; red alert/destructive, green go; status chips as printed lot tags; stamp only on confirm.

STORY: The visitor sees today's harvest with real per-kilo prices and live stock, trusts that the numbers come from the ledger, and adds to the basket in two taps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Stitched seam header, badge left, sack basket with count right. A full-width sack panel: "TODAY'S HARVEST" in huge condensed red caps beside a net-weight stamp carrying one featured variant's real seeded price. Below, three featured products as smaller sack labels (photo, price per variant, Add). Primary action: Add on each card.

FORM: Rice-sack flexo print, #1 on the ordered list (taken as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the rolled Bayong #5); seed key e391c476.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
