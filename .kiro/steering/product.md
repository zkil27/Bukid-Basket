# Product: Bukid Basket

Academic capstone (course code "SIA") made by **EJ and Kiel** for a **fictional**
farm-goods seller in Dasmariñas City, Cavite: vegetables & greens, fruits,
meat & poultry, eggs, rice & grains.

- **Customer storefront** — catalog by category and dish, live stock and price
  per pack, browser basket, checkout (GCash or COD, morning slot), order
  tracking and receipts, saved delivery details.
- **Admin** — confirm → send out → mark delivered, stock desk, append-only
  ledger, today's prices, products and categories.

No packer or driver roles, no trips, no handoff PIN. Read `AGENTS.md` for the
stack (vanilla PHP 8.2 + MySQL), the non-negotiable rules and the commands.
